# Iqama Renewal SOP (KSA)

Applies to all KSA-based employees holding a valid Iqama (residency permit).

## Timeline
1. **60 days before expiry:** HR sends a first reminder to the employee and their manager. The Iqama expiry check tool flags this as "notice" tier.
2. **30 days before expiry:** HR opens the renewal case with the government portal (Muqeem/Absher) and requests the employee's updated documents (passport copy, medical fitness if required). Flagged as "warning" tier.
3. **14 days before expiry:** Escalation to HR leadership if the renewal case isn't yet submitted. Flagged as "urgent" tier — this is the point where employment continuity is at real risk.
4. **On renewal:** HR updates the employee's Iqama number and new expiry date in the HRIS.

## Employee responsibilities
- Provide a valid passport with at least 6 months' validity remaining.
- Complete any required medical fitness test promptly when requested.
- Report any change of address or job title, since these affect the Iqama record.

## What happens if an Iqama lapses
An expired Iqama can restrict the employee's ability to work, travel, or access government services, and may expose the company to fines. Treat every "urgent" tier case as a same-day priority.
