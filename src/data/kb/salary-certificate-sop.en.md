# Salary Certificate Issuance SOP

Applies to all employees requesting a salary certificate (commonly needed for bank loans, visa applications, or embassy paperwork).

## Process
1. Employee submits a request stating the purpose (bank, embassy, visa, other) and the language required (English or Arabic).
2. HR verifies current employment status and monthly wage from the HRIS.
3. HR issues the certificate on official letterhead, signed and stamped, within 2 business days of the request.
4. The certificate states job title, hire date, and monthly wage as of the issuance date. It does not include bonus, commission, or variable pay unless the employee explicitly requests a "total compensation" version.

## Notes
- Certificates are valid for the purpose stated; a new certificate is required if the purpose changes.
- Bank-purpose certificates sometimes require a specific bank template — ask the employee to share the bank's format if they have one.
- This SOP does not currently have a self-service tool; requests are routed to HR to issue manually.
