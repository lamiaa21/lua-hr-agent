# Sick Leave Policy

Mock policy modelled on public labour-law summaries. Not legal advice.

## Saudi Arabia (KSA)
- First 30 days: full pay.
- Next 60 days: 75% pay.
- Final 30 days: unpaid.
- 120 days total per year across all three tiers.

## United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- First 15 days: full pay.
- Next 30 days: 50% pay.
- 45 days total per year.

## Egypt
- Approximated at 90 days at 75% pay followed by 90 days at 85% pay under the social insurance schedule (180 days total per year). Actual payout runs through social insurance, not payroll directly — direct affected employees to HR for the exact process.

## Jordan
- 14 days/year at full pay.

A medical certificate from a licensed provider is required to approve any sick leave request beyond a single day, in all four countries.
