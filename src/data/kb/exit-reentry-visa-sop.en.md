# Exit/Re-entry Visa SOP (KSA)

Applies to KSA-based expatriate employees who need to leave and return to the Kingdom while their Iqama remains valid.

## Process
1. Employee requests an exit/re-entry visa through HR, stating intended travel dates.
2. HR checks the employee's Iqama expiry — the return date on the visa cannot exceed the Iqama's validity. If the Iqama is expiring soon, the Iqama renewal SOP takes priority over booking travel.
3. HR submits the exit/re-entry request through the government portal (Absher/Muqeem) linked to the employee's Iqama.
4. HR confirms the approved visa and travel window back to the employee before they book flights.

## Types
- **Single exit/re-entry:** one trip, fixed validity window.
- **Multiple exit/re-entry:** longer validity, multiple trips — used for employees who travel frequently for work.

## Notes
- An employee who exits the country without a valid exit/re-entry visa, or fails to return before it expires, risks their Iqama being cancelled.
- Final exit (permanently leaving the company/country) is a separate process from exit/re-entry — do not confuse the two when an employee asks about "leaving."
