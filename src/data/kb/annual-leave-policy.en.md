# Annual Leave Policy

Mock policy modelled on public labour-law summaries for our four countries of operation. Not legal advice — for a specific employee's exact balance, use the leave balance tool, which applies these rules to their tenure.

## Saudi Arabia (KSA)
- 21 days/year for employees with under 5 years of service.
- 30 days/year once an employee passes 5 years of service.

## United Arab Emirates (UAE)
- No annual leave entitlement in the first 6 months of service.
- Between 6 and 12 months of service, leave accrues at 2 days per month.
- 30 calendar days/year once an employee passes 1 year of service.

## Egypt
- 21 days/year for employees under 10 years of service and under age 50.
- 30 days/year once an employee passes 10 years of service, or reaches age 50 — whichever comes first.

## Jordan
- 14 days/year for employees with under 5 years with the same employer.
- 21 days/year once an employee passes 5 years with the same employer.

## Probation and accrual
Annual leave does not accrue during the probation period in any of the four countries (see the Probation Policy document for probation lengths by country).
