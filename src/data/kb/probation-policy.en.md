# Probation Policy by Country

Mock policy modelled on public labour-law summaries. Not legal advice.

| Country | Probation length | Annual leave accrues during probation? |
|---|---|---|
| Saudi Arabia (KSA) | 90 days | No |
| United Arab Emirates (UAE) | 180 days | No |
| Egypt | 90 days | No |
| Jordan | 90 days | No |

During probation, either party may end employment with shorter notice than the standard notice period (see individual employment contracts for exact terms — this varies by role and isn't standardized centrally). Sick and emergency leave entitlements are unaffected by probation status.
