# End-of-Service Gratuity Policy (KSA)

Mock policy modelled on public labour-law summaries. Not legal advice. Currently modelled for Saudi Arabia only — other countries in our footprint aren't covered by this policy or the calculation tool yet.

## Base award
- Half a month's wage for each of the first 5 years of service.
- A full month's wage for each year of service beyond 5 years.
- Partial years are pro-rated.

## Payout by separation type
- **Termination or end of contract:** full award, regardless of tenure.
- **Resignation:**
  - Under 2 years of service: no entitlement.
  - 2 to 5 years of service: one third of the award.
  - 5 to 10 years of service: two thirds of the award.
  - 10+ years of service: full award.

For an exact figure with the full calculation shown, use the gratuity calculation tool rather than estimating by hand — it applies these rules to the employee's actual wage and tenure and shows the breakdown.
